# Code of conduct

This project's expectations are short, and inspired by the Contributor Covenant.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) is treated with respect, whatever their background, identity, experience or opinions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work, and admit your own mistakes.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and block participants, that break this code. Decisions are explained to the person concerned where it is safe to do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, or use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form ([private reporting](../../security/advisories/new)) if the matter is sensitive. Reports are kept confidential.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
